## 2. Categories of Information

The App may process the following types of information in connection with its features.

### 2.1 Training and health-related data

Workout sessions, exercises, sets, weight, reps, time, notes, and similar data entered by the user; body metrics where applicable.

### 2.2 Device and usage

App version, OS version, and device model may be used for compatibility and troubleshooting. Analytics events are primarily written to on-device logging; the default project configuration does not send them to third-party analytics backends automatically.

### 2.3 Purchases

Non-consumable in-app purchases ("Premium") are validated via Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

### 2.4 Apple Health

If the user enables "Save workouts to Health" in Settings, completed workouts may be written to Apple Health (HealthKit). If disabled, no such writes occur.

### 2.5 iCloud / CloudKit

The App may sync data using the user's private CloudKit database tied to their Apple ID. Availability depends on Apple services, device settings, and App behavior. Processing is also subject to Apple's privacy policies.

### 2.6 Notifications

With OS permission, the App may schedule local notifications (e.g., reminders).

### 2.7 Advertising

The App may use Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Ad delivery may involve identifiers and device-related data sent to Google and partners under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [AdMob-related disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy).
